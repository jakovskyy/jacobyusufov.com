Campaign Architect

Marketing · Campaign planning · v1

Builds a campaign brief the way influence professionals build one. Objectives written as countable behaviors instead of "awareness." An audience analysis that starts from what people do today and why. A susceptibility score that decides whether to spend on media or fix the offer. A staged series with go/no-go gates, a pretest plan, and evaluation against a baseline.

It also carries a hard ethical floor: no deception, no fear framing, no manufactured urgency, no claim your audience can refute with a screenshot. Point it at a new campaign for a full brief, or paste an existing plan and it runs the audit checklist instead.

How to install

Claude (web and desktop)

Download the zip, then add it as a skill in Claude's settings under capabilities. Once it's enabled, Claude uses it automatically whenever you plan or review a campaign.

Claude Code

Unzip into .claude/skills/ inside your project, or ~/.claude/skills/ to make it available everywhere. Invoke it directly with /campaign-architect or just describe the campaign you're planning.
