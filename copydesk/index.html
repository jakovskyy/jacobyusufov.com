How it works

What the desk keeps track of

One folder per client

House rules, brand, the upkeep log, the pipeline, the campaign briefs. Plain markdown with a few lines of front matter. Open them in anything. Version them in git if you like.

One pack per industry

A pack is the rules that change when the industry does: the claims that need proof, the words that are banned, the regulations that apply. A client declares its packs, and the upkeep agent reads them on every pass, so the rules are followed and the facts stay accurate.

Every piece keeps its record

Because this is a copywriter's desk, a piece keeps its full working record and not just the prose. The SEO fields live in the front matter, and the same file carries the JSON-LD schema, the FAQ block that becomes FAQPage markup, and the llms.txt entry that tells AI search engines which page to cite. When an answer engine quotes your client, it quotes the page you chose.

Who does the work

Two agents and you

U

The upkeep agent

The upkeep agent keeps everything you have already published accurate. When a rule changes, like a product name or a claim the client no longer makes, it rereads every piece against the new rules. That is the rules pass. When a fact changes, like a price or a carrier list, it finds the sentences that carry that fact and checks only those. That is the fact sweep.

It checks the SEO record the same way it checks the copy, because a meta description that still sells last quarter's offer is just as wrong as a paragraph that does.

C

Campaign Architect

Campaign Architect writes the campaign brief the way influence professionals write one. It sets objectives as behaviors you can count instead of awareness goals, and it analyzes the audience by starting from what those people already do and why. Then it scores how open they actually are to being moved, which tells you whether to spend on media or fix the offer instead.

It works under a hard ethical floor: no deception, no fear framing, no manufactured urgency, and no claim your audience can refute with a screenshot.

✓

You

Everything the agents produce comes to you before it goes anywhere. You approve, modify or override every flag and every draft, and nothing is published, patched or retired without you.

The agents handle the checking and the analysis. The judgment stays yours, along with the client relationship and the byline.

Why it exists

One person, all the desks.

Newsrooms solved content quality with a chain of desks. The writer filed, the editor shaped the story, the copy desk checked every name, figure and claim, the SEO editor handled search, and an editor in chief permitted the piece through. Then the industry spent a decade cutting those desks to save money, and the checks landed on whoever was left. Anyone writing for clients today works in that same condition: one person doing the work of every desk, with no chain behind them.

Copy Desk gives that chain back as checks one person can run, without a server, an account, or a subscription. Install it, add a client, and the first sweep tells you what is already wrong.
